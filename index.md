# Mental Health Patterns in Canada and the United States, 2000–2021

### A Multivariate Analysis Using R

**Abbas Ali**
Statistics Honours Student | University of Manitoba

---

## Project Overview

This project examines mental health patterns in **Canada and the United States from 2000 to 2021** using multivariate statistical analysis in R.

The analysis explores trends, relationships, and differences in mental health indicators across the two countries while demonstrating reproducible statistical research using R and R Markdown.

---

## Research Objectives

* Compare mental health patterns in Canada and the United States.
* Examine changes in mental health indicators from 2000 to 2021.
* Apply multivariate statistical methods to identify important relationships and patterns.
* Communicate results through statistical analysis and data visualization.
